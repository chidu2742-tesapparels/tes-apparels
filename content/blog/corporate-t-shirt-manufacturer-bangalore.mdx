Bangalore has a strong apparel and garment manufacturing ecosystem, with many manufacturers and suppliers offering customized corporate clothing. However, not every **corporate T-shirt manufacturer** provides the same level of product quality, customization, production capacity or service.

## 6. Understand GSM Before Ordering

GSM stands for grams per square metre and is commonly used to describe fabric weight.

However, higher GSM does not automatically mean higher quality.

The appropriate GSM depends on:

* Fabric composition
* T-shirt design
* Climate
* Intended use
* Comfort requirements
* Desired appearance
* Budget

For example, lightweight T-shirts may be suitable for promotional events, while heavier fabrics may be preferred for premium corporate apparel.

Instead of asking only for the highest GSM, ask manufacturers to recommend a suitable specification for your application.

## 9. Printing Options for Corporate T-Shirts

The right branding method depends on the artwork, quantity, fabric and desired appearance.

### Screen Printing

Screen printing is widely used for bulk T-shirt orders.

It can be suitable for designs with strong colour coverage and relatively consistent artwork.

### DTF Printing

DTF printing can be useful for detailed and colourful designs.

It may be considered for customized corporate apparel where the artwork contains multiple colours or fine details.

### Heat Transfer

Heat transfer methods can be useful for certain names, numbers and customized graphics.

### Sublimation

Sublimation is generally associated with polyester fabrics and is commonly used for sportswear.

### Embroidery

Embroidery can provide a premium and professional appearance.

It is particularly popular for:

* Corporate logos
* Polo T-shirts
* Uniforms
* Hospitality apparel
* Staff clothing

When comparing **corporate T-shirt manufacturers**, ask which branding methods they provide and which method they recommend for your specific fabric and artwork.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I look for in a corporate T-shirt manufacturer in Bangalore?

Look at fabric quality, garment construction, sizing consistency, customization capability, printing and embroidery options, production capacity, quality control, MOQ, pricing and delivery reliability.

### Which T-shirt is best for corporate employees?

The best T-shirt depends on the workplace and intended use. Round-neck T-shirts are popular for casual corporate activities, while polo T-shirts are often preferred for professional uniforms and customer-facing teams.

### What fabric is suitable for corporate T-shirts?

Cotton, cotton blends and polyester fabrics can all be suitable depending on the intended use. The manufacturer should recommend the fabric based on comfort, durability, climate, branding and budget.

### Should I choose printing or embroidery for a company logo?

Both can work well. Embroidery is often preferred for corporate polo T-shirts and uniforms, while printing can be more suitable for larger graphics and event-specific designs.

### What is MOQ for corporate T-shirts?

MOQ varies between manufacturers and depends on fabric, colour, product type, customization and production requirements. Always confirm the MOQ before placing an order.

### Can corporate T-shirt manufacturers handle bulk orders?

Many experienced manufacturers handle bulk corporate orders. Before selecting a supplier, confirm their production capacity, lead time and ability to maintain consistent quality.

### Can I order different sizes in the same corporate T-shirt order?

Yes, corporate orders commonly contain multiple sizes. Provide the manufacturer with an approved size chart and size-wise quantity requirement.

### Can a corporate T-shirt manufacturer deliver outside Bangalore?

Many manufacturers can arrange delivery across Karnataka and other parts of India. Confirm transportation costs and delivery timelines before placing the order.

### How early should I order corporate T-shirts for an event?

The required lead time depends on quantity, fabric, customization and production requirements. For fixed-date events, it is advisable to discuss the requirement with the manufacturer well in advance and allow time for sampling and approval.
